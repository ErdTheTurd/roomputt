Roomputt

The couch is a bank. The coffee table is in the way.

Mini golf on the floor of your living room. You place a tee and a cup, then pinch and pull back to putt.

Coming soon to Apple Vision Pro. In development, not released.

What it does

Planned for launch. Roomputt has not shipped.

  • The room

    Look around your living room. The floor, the walls, and the furniture stay where they are and become the hole.

  • The couch

    The couch becomes a bank shot. The ball can come off it and keep rolling.

  • The coffee table

    The coffee table is the obstacle. The path bends around it.

  • Tee and cup

    You place the tee and the cup on your floor.

  • The putt

    Aim, pinch, and pull back to putt.

  • The scorecard

    Each hole keeps a stroke count.

Holes and strokes

You pick the hole. The scorecard counts the strokes for each one.

Privacy

Roomputt is designed to work offline.
