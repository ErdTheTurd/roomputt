Roomputt

Mini golf played in your real room, with holes built around your own furniture.

How it works

A hole made from the room you are in.

  1. 1

    Scan your room

    The floor, walls, and furniture become the course.

  2. 2

    Place the tee and the cup

    Set them around the furniture already in the room.

  3. 3

    Aim and putt

    Aim and putt with a pinch gesture.
